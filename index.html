Vietnamese Street Food

A short guide to three dishes you should try.

Top Three Dishes

  1. Pho
  2. Banh mi
  3. Goi cuon (fresh spring rolls)

Dish Gallery

Pho

Pho is a noodle soup with beef or chicken broth and herbs.

Banh mi

Banh mi is a crispy baguette filled with meat, pickles, and herbs.

Goi cuon

Goi cuon are rice paper rolls with shrimp, vegetables, and noodles.
